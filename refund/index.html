Refund Policy

Last updated:

The Krishnaveni School Digital Library is free to use. We do not sell anything on this website and we do not collect any payment, fee, donation or card details.

What this means

  • Reading and downloading books costs nothing.
  • Because no money is taken, there is nothing to refund.
  • If anyone asks you to pay for access to this library, it is not from the school. Please tell us.

If this changes

If the school ever offers a paid service on this website, we will publish clear prices and a full refund and cancellation policy here before taking any payment.
