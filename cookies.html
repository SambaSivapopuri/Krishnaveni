Cookie Policy

Last updated:

Cookies are small files that websites can store in your browser. This website does not set any cookies. It uses a little essential browser storage so that it can work, listed below.

What we use

NameTypePurposeHow long
kv-adminSession storage (essential, staff only)Keeps a staff member signed in on the admin page.Until the tab is closed
kv-cookie-consentLocal storage (essential)Remembers your choice on the cookie notice.Until you clear browser data
krishnaveni-libraryIndexedDB (essential, staff only)Keeps documents and list changes made by staff on that computer.Until deleted by staff or browser data is cleared

What we do not use

  • No advertising cookies.
  • No analytics or tracking cookies.
  • No cookies from other companies (Google, Facebook and similar).

Your choice

Essential storage is needed for the website to work. Optional cookies are not used at the moment; if we ever add them (for example visitor statistics), they will stay switched off unless you press “Accept optional” on the cookie notice. You can change your choice any time:

You can also delete stored data in your browser settings.
