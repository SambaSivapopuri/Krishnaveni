Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains how the Krishnaveni School Digital Library (“the website”) treats information. It is written to follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”). The website is made for school students, many of them under 18, so we keep data collection to the absolute minimum.

2. What we collect

From students and visitors: no personal data. The website has no sign-up, no student accounts, no comment boxes, no contact forms and no payment. We do not ask for names, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, photos or locations.

Like any website, the company that hosts the site may keep standard technical server logs (for example IP address and page requested) for security. We do not use these logs to identify or profile students.

3. No tracking, no advertising

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or social-media plug-ins.
  • No behavioural monitoring of children and no targeted advertising aimed at children.
  • All fonts and scripts are hosted on this website itself, so your browser does not contact Google or other companies when you read a book.

4. Children

The DPDP Act requires verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian before the personal data of a child (under 18) is processed. Because this website collects no personal data from children, no such consent is needed today. If the school ever adds a feature that collects a child’s personal data (for example student log-in or quizzes), we will first obtain verifiable parental or guardian consent and update this policy.

5. Staff documents

Staff must not upload documents that contain personal details of students or staff (for example marks lists with names, photos or addresses). The upload form asks the staff member to confirm this.

6. Storage and security

Information kept in a browser stays there until it is deleted from the admin page or the browser data is cleared. The website is plain static files and does not run a database. We recommend serving it over HTTPS.

7. Your rights under the DPDP Act

You, or the parent or guardian of a child, may ask the school to give information about personal data processed, correct or update it, erase it, make a grievance, or nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to. Write to the contact above. If your grievance is not resolved, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the “last updated” date whenever our practices change.
